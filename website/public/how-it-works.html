Home/How it works

AI does the heavy lifting. A person you can call owns every decision.

Here's how BYLD connects to your hotel, how the models and your revenue manager work together, and how you stay in the loop without learning a new tool.

01Machine

AI and ML models

Read your PMS pace, booking curves, competitor rates and market data, then forecast demand and recommend rates for every room.

02Rules you set

Guardrails

Floors, ceilings and your business rules are enforced on every recommendation. No model can cross them.

03Your RM

Human revenue manager

Reviews each recommendation with its full reasoning, adjusts where judgement says so, and approves, in the portal or on WhatsApp.

04Results

Live and measured

Approved rates go live. Every decision is logged and scored afterwards, so the models and the team keep learning.

Under the hood

Connected to your data. Running while you sleep.

Every morning the platform pulls your PMS, the comp set and the market, checks the data is fresh, and has recommendations waiting for your revenue manager.

FAQ

Questions owners ask

Straight answers. If yours isn't here, ask us.

Talk to our team
Do I need to change my PMS?

No. You can run BFIS PMS, our cloud PMS, or keep your current system. We already take feeds from ASI, G6, IHG and others, and can connect a direct booking engine to any PMS in about a week.

Does AI set my prices on its own?

Never. Our models forecast and recommend, guardrails keep every number inside your floors and ceilings, and a named human revenue manager approves every rate before it goes live.

What kinds of hotels do you work with?

Branded and independent hotels. Our portfolio includes properties flying Red Roof, Sonesta, Choice, Wyndham, Hilton, Marriott and IHG flags, as well as independents.
