What it does

KeyCircle™, feature by feature.

01

A personal thank-you from your GM

Sent the moment an account is won or renewed, with a QR code: “Please give us an opportunity to thank you the way you deserve.”

02

A profile they choose to share

Interests, favourite team, key dates and family. Everything but their name is optional, and it's stored with their consent.

03

Every booking linked

Corporate rate codes, company profiles and group blocks from BFIS PMS roll up to the person, so you see the business each one brings.

04

Four tiers

Platinum, Gold, Silver and Emerging, recalculated each quarter from the last 12 months of linked business.

05

Gestures at the right moment

Birthdays, anniversaries, their team's big game, a business milestone, or a check-in when business dips.

06

Within their gift policy

Every gesture is logged and kept under the limit their company allows. No gifts tied to a pending contract.

FAQ

KeyCircle™ questions

Anything else, ask your revenue manager or our team.

Talk to our team
Who counts as a key influencer?

The person who decides which hotel their company uses: often a travel manager, executive assistant, procurement lead or event planner.

How is their business measured?

From BFIS PMS. Reservations on their company profile, negotiated rate code or group blocks are linked automatically; likely matches are confirmed by your Director of Sales.

Is this allowed under corporate gift policies?

KeyCircle asks each person about their company's gift limit and keeps every gesture under it, logs everything, and limits public-sector buyers to thank-you notes.

What happens to the personal details they share?

They're used only to personalise your thanks, seen only by your GM, owner and Director of Sales, and deleted on request.
