A loyalty program with your hotel's name on it: “[Your Hotel] GoldKey Rewards”. Guests join at the front desk, get a personal welcome and a metal-look member card, and come back through your own booking engine instead of an OTA. No per-stay brand loyalty fees.

GoldKey™ Rewards, feature by feature.

30-second sign-up

By QR code at the front desk or the restaurant table.

A welcome that feels personal

Your hotel's photo, their name, their member card and their reward, by email.

Rewards matched to the stay

15% off the next direct stay, free nights, or a restaurant credit.

GoldKey™ Rewards questions

Anything else, ask your revenue manager or our team.

Talk to our team
Whose brand is on the program?

Yours. Members join “[Your Hotel] GoldKey Rewards”, with your hotel's photo and name.

Can rewards be used on OTA bookings?

No. Member codes work only on your own booking engine, which is the point: members come back direct.

Does it work across several hotels?

Yes. Stays at your hotels count together, and free nights work at any of them.
