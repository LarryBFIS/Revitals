Talk to our team
Do I have to switch to BFIS PMS to work with BYLD?

No. BFIS PMS is our own system and the fastest route, but we also connect to the PMS you already run, including ASI, G6 and IHG feeds.

Can one login cover several hotels?

Yes. BFIS PMS is multi-property: owners and managers switch between hotels from a single login.
