PriceCortex™The right rate for every room, 57 nights ahead.

Every day, for every hotel, PriceCortex reads where the books are against where they should be, checks the market, and recommends a rate for each room category. Your revenue manager reviews the reasoning and approves. Nothing reaches your guests without a person signing off.

What it does

PriceCortex™, feature by feature.

01

On the books vs target vs last year

Rooms sold today, the target from your booking curve, and last year's final on the same day of the week.

02

Pace against the booking curve

How many rooms ahead or behind you are, so price moves are driven by real demand.

03

A rate per room category, inside your limits

Always between a floor and a ceiling, with each band showing where it came from: your rule, your listing, your PMS rate or the market.

04

RevPAR and demand pressure

Recent RevPAR against target and last year, plus a 1-to-5 demand-pressure score.

AI + human oversight

The model recommends. A person decides.

01Machine

AI and ML models

Read your PMS pace, booking curves, competitor rates and market data, then forecast demand and recommend rates for every room.

02Rules you set

Guardrails

Floors, ceilings and your business rules are enforced on every recommendation. No model can cross them.

03Your RM

Human revenue manager

Reviews each recommendation with its full reasoning, adjusts where judgement says so, and approves, in the portal or on WhatsApp.

04Results

Live and measured

Approved rates go live. Every decision is logged and scored afterwards, so the models and the team keep learning.

PriceCortex™ questions

Anything else, ask your revenue manager or our team.

Talk to our team
Does PriceCortex change my rates automatically?

No. PriceCortex recommends; a named human revenue manager reviews the reasoning and approves before anything goes live. Floors and ceilings you set can never be crossed.

How far ahead does it price?

57 nights ahead, for every room category, every day.

Can I see why a rate was recommended?

Yes. Every recommendation carries its audit trail: the pace signal, the competitor position and the guardrail that applied.
