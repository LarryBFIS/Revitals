What it does

OTARecon365™, feature by feature.

01

Full-window audit

Up to 365 days on Booking.com and 180 on Expedia, to find bookings never paid or under-paid.

02

Virtual cards tracked

Pending virtual cards tracked against each OTA's charge deadline, so none expire uncollected.

03

Your money, your account

Cards are charged through Stripe to the hotel's own account. We never see a card number.

04

Performance-based

Our fee is a percentage of the recoverable amount we find, invoiced per hotel.

How far back does OTARecon365 look?

Up to 365 days on Booking.com and 180 days on Expedia.

Does BYLD handle card numbers?

No. Cards are charged through Stripe to the hotel's own account and we never see a card number.

How is it priced?

Performance-based: a percentage of the recoverable amount we find, invoiced per hotel.
