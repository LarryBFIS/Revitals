What do we do?

Revenue work is scattered. We bring it under one roof.

Most hotels piece revenue management together from a PMS, a pricing tool, a rate shopper, a BI dashboard, an OTA marketing agency, a reputation tool and a stack of spreadsheets. We run the whole cycle on one data foundation, starting with our own PMS, so every decision uses the same numbers and every result is reported in one place.

BFIS PMS
Where every revenue decision starts.
Live

Our cloud property management system, running live hotels today. The tape chart shows every room, every night and every guest at a glance, with room status, folios, rates and reports one click away, and its data flows straight into pricing, reporting and loyalty.

AI + human oversight

Advanced AI and machine learning. Guarded by a human Revenue Manager.

Machines are fast at reading thousands of signals. People are good at judgement. BYLD uses both: our models forecast and recommend, hard guardrails keep every number inside your limits, and a dedicated Revenue Manager reviews and approves before anything reaches your guests.

PriceCortex™
The right rate for every room, 57 nights ahead.
LiveAI + human RM
Developed by BYLD

Every day, for every hotel, PriceCortex™ reads where the books are against where they should be, checks the market, and recommends a rate for each room category. Your Revenue Manager reviews the reasoning and approves.

1
On the books vs where they should be vs last year: rooms sold today, the target from your booking curve, and last year's final on the same day of the week.
2
Pace against the booking curve: how many rooms ahead or behind you are, so price moves are driven by real demand.
3
Recommended rate per room category, always inside a floor and ceiling, and each band shows where it came from: your rule, your own listing, your PMS rate or the market.
4
RevPAR and demand pressure: recent RevPAR vs target and last year, and a 1-to-5 demand-pressure score.

OTARecon365™
Collect what you're owed.
Live
Developed by BYLD

Every OTA payout audited booking by booking across its full collection window, and the missing money chased until it lands in your account.

1
Full-window audit: up to 365 days on Booking.com and 180 on Expedia, to find bookings never paid or under-paid.
2
Pending virtual cards tracked against each OTA's charge deadline, so none expire uncollected.
3
Your money, your account: cards are charged through Stripe to the hotel's own account, and we never see a card number.
4
Performance-based: our fee is a percentage of the recoverable amount we find, invoiced per hotel.
