When it's sent
1Account won or renewed. The Director of Sales marks the decision maker on the company profile in BFIS PMS or SalesLock™.
2GM approves in one tap. The email is pre-written with the company, the occasion and the person's name. The GM can add a personal line.
3Unique QR per person. The code carries a private token, so the profile page already knows who they are and which account they manage.
4Gentle reminder. If the profile isn't completed in 10 days, one follow-up goes out, then nothing more.
5A small welcome. Completing the profile triggers a handwritten card and a small first gesture, picked from what they told us.
Step 3 · Profile

What the QR code opens

A short, warm, mobile page. Only the name and the consent are required. Everything else is optional, so people share what they're comfortable with. Try it: submitting creates a live profile in the owner portal (tab 3).

Design choices
Optional by default. Family, address and dates are clearly optional. Asking politely, not pushing, keeps the gesture feeling like thanks rather than data collection.
Consent is explicit and recorded. It's timestamped against the profile, with a self-service link to view, edit or delete.
Gift policy asked up front. Many companies cap what employees can accept. Knowing their limit means every gesture can be accepted without putting them in an awkward spot.
Their preference wins. Some prefer experiences (a dinner with family, match tickets), others a quiet note. The page asks, so the hotel doesn't guess.
Pre-filled from the QR token. Company and account are already known, so the page feels personal and takes about two minutes.

Step 5–6 · Tier & gratify

Categories based on the business they bring

Tiers are recalculated each quarter from the last 12 months of business linked to each person. The appreciation budget scales with value, and every single gesture stays within the person's company gift limit.

Platinum Circle

$150K+ · 12 months
  • Personal thank-you from the owner each year
  • Birthday and anniversary gestures
  • Family dinner or weekend stay, as an experience
  • Tickets to their team's game or a film premiere
  • Quarterly check-in from the GM
Suggested budget: up to $1,500 / yr

Gold

$50K–150K · 12 months
  • Birthday gesture matched to interests
  • Dinner for two in the hotel restaurant
  • Holiday hamper sent home
  • Twice-yearly GM check-in
Suggested budget: up to $750 / yr

Silver

$15K–50K · 12 months
  • Birthday card and small gift
  • Holiday greeting with a treat
  • Invitation to hotel events
Suggested budget: up to $300 / yr

Emerging

Under $15K · or new
  • Thank-you email and handwritten card
  • Birthday card
  • Watched for growth to Silver
Suggested budget: up to $100 / yr

Guardrails built in
Company gift limit enforced. The portal won't schedule a gesture above the limit they told us. "No gifts" switches them to experiences or notes only.
Thanks after the decision, never before. Gestures reward a relationship that already exists; they are never offered to win a contract or tied to a pending RFP.
Every gesture logged. Date, item, value and who approved it, so the hotel can show its program is above board if a client ever asks.
No government or public-sector buyers. Accounts flagged as public sector get thank-you notes only. Stricter anti-bribery rules apply there.
Privacy by design. Consent recorded, data visible only to the GM, owner and Director of Sales, and deleted on request or two years after the account goes inactive.
Step 4 · Attribute

Linking every booking to the person who made it happen

The value of each influencer comes from the PMS, not from guesswork. Each person is linked to one or more corporate accounts, and every reservation on those accounts rolls up to their profile automatically.

How a booking is matched
SignalSourceConfidence
Company profile on the reservationBFIS PMSExact
Negotiated rate code (LNR)BFIS PMS / rate plansExact
Group block from that companySalesLock™ / PMS group masterExact
Booker email domain (e.g. @northwind.example)Booking engine, OTA, emailHigh: auto-suggested
Guest's company field on folioFolioLens™Medium: reviewed by sales

Exact matches are linked automatically. High and medium matches go into a weekly review queue for the Director of Sales to confirm in one tap.

What's counted
Room revenue and room nights from transient corporate stays and group blocks, net of cancellations.
Meetings and F&B booked under the same account.
Rolling 12 months versus the prior 12, so growth and dips show clearly.
Several people per account. If a travel manager and an EA both influence an account, revenue can be split, for example 70/30.
People who move companies. The profile follows the person. When they join a new company, sales is alerted to win that account too.
Feeds the rest of BYLD: attributed revenue shows in RevVitals reports, at-risk accounts surface in PulsePilot™, and new accounts from SalesLock™ prompt the thank-you email automatically.
