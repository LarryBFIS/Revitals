What it means

Offer strength fell below Expedia's 80-point Milestone threshold and the platform responded exactly as designed: it revoked milestone and VIP-Access benefits, demoted sort placement, and stopped showing the property. Fewer eyeballs → fewer bookings → weaker signals → fewer eyeballs. The spiral is algorithmic, which is the good news — it reverses on the same mechanics it decayed on.

Promotion share at 34% vs a compset running 59% means shoppers see this property undiscounted next to nine discounted rivals. Combined with an ADR $6 under the set, the property is somehow both cheaper and less visible — the worst available trade.

2025 closed at $295K on this channel; 2026 is tracking $109K. Nothing in the product explains that — Booking.com proves the hotel converts when it's seen.
