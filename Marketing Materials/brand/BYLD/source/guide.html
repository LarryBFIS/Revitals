Buildd From Ideas

BYLD is the mark of Buildd From Ideas.

The company name stays Buildd From Ideas in writing, contracts and email. BYLD is the short mark for swag, signage, social and the app icon. The arrow in the Y rises: we build revenue upward.

The logo always sits on a dark background.

At 16 px (browser tabs) the letters blur into a shape. That is expected.

Keep it clean

Only on dark backgrounds: Night, black or a dark photo area.

Never on white or light colours.

Don't stretch, rotate or recolour it.

No shadows, glows or outlines.

Don't retype BYLD in a font. Use the files.

Don't move or recolour the arrow.

For developers & printers

SVGs are outlined paths: no fonts, no strokes, no images. Logo viewBox 0 -24 394 124, cropped tight. On-dark viewBox -50 -74 494 224 includes clear space. Icon viewBox 0 0 200 200, radius 56. PNGs are transparent except the on-dark set. PDFs are vector. source/ rebuilds everything.
